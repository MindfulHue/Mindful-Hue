🌿 General Questions

Mindful Hue is a wellness education platform and community for late-identified and self-identified neurodivergent Black women. We offer a podcast, The Hue Collective membership community, and digital wellness resources — all built around the experiences of Black women navigating ADHD, late diagnoses, masking, and the exhaustion of living in systems not built for us.
No. Mindful Hue is a wellness education platform — not a therapy practice. Our content is for educational and informational purposes only and does not constitute therapy, diagnosis, or clinical treatment. Engaging with Mindful Hue does not create a therapeutic relationship. If you need clinical support please reach out to a licensed mental health professional in your area.
Myra is the founder and host of Mindful Hue. She is a Licensed Clinical Social Worker (LCSW) and a late-identified neurodivergent Black woman who built this platform because the space she needed didn't exist. She brings both clinical expertise and lived experience to everything Mindful Hue creates.
Mindful Hue was built specifically for Black women navigating neurodivergence and mental wellness. Our content, community, and voice center the experiences of Black women. While our podcast is publicly available and anyone is welcome to listen, The Hue Collective membership community is designed as a space specifically for Black women.

✦ The Hue Collective Membership

The Hue Collective is our private membership community — a curated, intimate space for late-diagnosed and self-identified neurodivergent Black women. Members get monthly group sessions with Myra, bonus podcast episodes, a clinical tools and resource library, access to the private community, monthly wellness resource drops, and a monthly Q&A.
The Hue Collective membership is $37 per month for founding members. This founding rate is locked in for as long as your membership remains active and uninterrupted — meaning you'll never pay more than $37/month even as the price increases for new members.
Yes — you can cancel anytime with no penalty. Your membership will remain active until the end of your current billing period. To cancel email us at care@mindfulhue.com and we will take care of it within 1 business day.
We offer a 7-day satisfaction guarantee for new members. If you join and are not satisfied within your first 7 days, contact us at care@mindfulhue.com for a full refund. After 7 days all charges are final.
The Hue Collective is launching soon. Join the waitlist on our homepage to be the first to know and to secure founding member pricing. Founding members who join at launch lock in the $37/month rate forever.

🎙️ The Podcast

The Mindful Hue Podcast is coming soon. Join our waitlist at the bottom of the homepage to be notified the moment it drops. We'll also be sharing bonus episodes with Hue Collective members before the public launch.
The Mindful Hue Podcast will be available on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and all major podcast platforms. Bonus and early-access episodes will be available exclusively to Hue Collective members.
The podcast will cover late ADHD and autism diagnoses in Black women, the experience of masking, emotional dysregulation, nervous system healing, relationships and neurodivergence, navigating systems not built for us, and real clinical tools translated into everyday language. No sugarcoating. No performing wellness. Just honest conversation.
