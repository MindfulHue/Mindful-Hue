Section 01

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Mindful Hue we understand that the women who find us are often in a vulnerable and tender place. Privacy is not a legal checkbox for us — it is a core value. We are committed to being transparent about what we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

This Privacy Policy explains how Mindful Hue collects, uses, and safeguards your personal information when you visit our website, subscribe to our email list, or become a member of The Hue Collective community.

The short version: We collect only what we need, we protect it carefully, and we will never sell it. Ever.

Section 02

Information We Collect

Information you give us directly:

  • Email address — when you join our waitlist or subscribe to our newsletter
  • Name — when you create a membership account
  • Payment information — when you subscribe to The Hue Collective (processed securely by our payment provider — we do not store your card details)
  • Any information you voluntarily share in community discussions or when contacting us

Information collected automatically:

  • Basic website analytics — pages visited, time on site, general location (country/region level only)
  • Device type and browser information
  • IP address (anonymized where possible)

Section 03

How We Use Your Information

We use your information only for the following purposes:

  • To send you emails you signed up for — newsletter, podcast launch notifications, member updates
  • To manage your membership account and process payments
  • To provide access to The Hue Collective community and member content
  • To respond to your questions and support requests
  • To improve our Platform based on how people use it
  • To comply with legal obligations

We will never use your information for purposes beyond what is described above without your explicit consent.

Section 04

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. These measures include encrypted data transmission, secure password practices, and limiting access to personal data to only those who need it to operate the Platform.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you have concerns about your account security please contact us immediately at care@mindfulhue.com.

Section 05

We Never Sell Your Data

Mindful Hue does not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes. Full stop. This is a core value of our community — not just a legal clause.

Section 06

Third Party Services

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to operate our Platform. These include:

  • Email platform — to send newsletters and member communications
  • Payment processor — to handle membership payments securely
  • Community platform — to host The Hue Collective private community
  • Podcast host — to distribute the Mindful Hue Podcast

These services may collect limited information as necessary to perform their functions. We choose partners who share our commitment to privacy and data protection. We do not share your information with any third party beyond what is necessary to operate these core services.

Section 07

Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies — small files stored on your device — to help the site function properly and to understand basic usage patterns. We do not use advertising cookies or track you across other websites.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Note that doing so may affect some functionality of our website.

Section 08

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link in any email we send
  • Cancel your membership at any time

To exercise any of these rights please contact us at care@mindfulhue.com. We will respond within 5 business days.

Section 09

Children's Privacy

Mindful Hue is intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information please contact us immediately at care@mindfulhue.com and we will delete it promptly.

Section 10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and notify active members via email for any material changes.

Your continued use of the Platform after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
